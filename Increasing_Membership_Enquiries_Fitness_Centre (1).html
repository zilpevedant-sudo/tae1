7. Business Improvement Suggestions

Suggestions for increasing membership enquiries through digital marketing

1. Improve Local Online Presence

Keep correct information about the fitness centre, location, contact details, programs and working hours available online.

2. Create More Workout Videos

Use short videos to show workouts, equipment, trainer guidance and the fitness centre environment.

3. Collect Member Reviews

Encourage members to share genuine reviews and fitness experiences to build trust with potential members.

4. Promote Trial Sessions

Encourage interested people to enquire and visit the centre through trial-session or introductory offers.

5. Respond Quickly to Enquiries

Use phone and WhatsApp to provide fast answers about membership fees, timings and available programs.

6. Track Enquiry Results

Track social media enquiries, website visits, calls and membership conversions to identify effective marketing activities.
